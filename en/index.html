Work

LovOn · 2024 – now · Mobile lead

An AI relationship app with real-time voice, grown from zero to 60K monthly users.

LovOn had to make emotionally sensitive conversations, a probabilistic AI backend and live voice feel predictable. I built the mobile foundation, led a team of three mobile engineers and worked directly with backend, product, design and marketing.

  1. 01Native iOS app on SwiftUI and UIKit, then the Android client.
  2. 02Real-time voice with the AI assistant via LiveKit and RetellAI.
  3. 03Multi-tier subscriptions: StoreKit, RevenueCat, later Adapty.
  4. 04A React Native web funnel for paid traffic: first AI session and purchase on the web, then a seamless hand-off into the app by deep link.
  5. 05Onboarding and rendering work that lifted engagement by 25%.

APEX BANK · 2023 – 2024 · iOS engineer

A native mobile bank, built from prototypes to launch. 20K customers in the first three months.

In banking every ambiguous state costs money. Loading, retries, duplicate actions, authorization and partial failure all had to behave visibly and consistently, and the codebase had to stay maintainable as the product surface grew.

  1. 01Banking and transaction flows in Swift, UIKit and SwiftUI.
  2. 02Micro-modular architecture on VIPER, later migrated to MVP-C with SPM packages split by layer.
  3. 03Networking layer and bank API integration, sensitive data in Keychain.
  4. 04Large product team, mandatory code review and test coverage.
Read the case study apexbank.uz ↗

A studio that ships utility apps fast. The job was a repeatable path from a short brief to the App Store without turning every codebase into a copy of the last one. This is where the 3–4 week MVP offer above comes from.

  1. 01Reusable UI library (UIKit, SwiftUI) and a base architecture template.

Questions

How much does an MVP cost and how long does it take?

Most MVPs land between $3,500 and $6,000 and take 3–4 weeks: app, backend, auth, subscriptions, analytics and App Store release. The exact number comes after a 20-minute call and a written scope.

Do you build the backend too?

Yes. For most MVPs that means Cloudflare Workers, Firebase or Supabase. If you already have an API, I integrate with it.

iOS only, or Android as well?

Native iOS is my main stack. For both platforms at once I use React Native, which is how LovOn’s web funnel and several client products were built. I also shipped LovOn’s Android client.

How does payment work?

50% before work starts, 50% on release. Monthly retainers are paid at the start of each month. Invoices come from a registered sole proprietorship; bank transfer, Wise or crypto.

What does “AI-native” mean in practice?

I use Claude Code and Codex as part of the build. I design the architecture and the data model, split the work into tasks, review every line that comes back and test the result. You get senior-level output at a pace a single engineer couldn’t hit by hand.

Can you join an existing team instead?

Yes, on a monthly retainer or a fixed scope. I’ve worked in a large banking team with code review and tests, and led a mobile team of three at LovOn.

Where are you and what hours do you work?

Tashkent, UTC+5. I overlap with European mornings and afternoons fully, and with US East Coast mornings.
