Context

The interface had to make discovery fast and intuitive while coordinating remote data, recommendations, user state, and rich content.

The challenge was keeping the mechanic light without making the implementation brittle.

Responsibilities

  • Built and launched the client-server iOS app from scratch.
  • Implemented discovery, recommendation, and supporting flows.
  • Organized state with MVVM and integrated REST APIs and Firebase.
  • Worked on performance and stability at consumer scale.
  • Connected the playful interaction model to dependable data and state.
200K+ installs.
200K+Installs · movie discovery · recommendation flows · consumer scale

Engineering decisions

One clear mechanic

Emoji and mood were the distinctive entry point into movie and TV discovery.

Separate state from presentation

MVVM kept content, selections, and view behavior understandable.

Design for imperfect networks

Provide useful loading, empty, retry, and cached states.

Measure real behavior

Firebase and production signals informed stability and usage decisions.
